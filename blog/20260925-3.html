Regional Impact

The blackout affects northern Ethiopia, primarily the Tigray region, potentially disrupting regional connectivity and affecting any cross-border traffic that relies on terrestrial infrastructure passing through the area.

Aleiwa Analysis

A nationwide or regional internet shutdown severely undermines network resilience and can have cascading effects on dependent services, including financial transactions, humanitarian coordination, and emergency communications. In conflict zones, such outages often reflect either infrastructure destruction or deliberate censorship, both of which impede restoration efforts and increase reliance on alternative routes such as satellite links, if available. The incident highlights the vulnerability of terrestrial networks in unstable regions and underscores the importance of diversified connectivity paths for maintaining baseline internet access.
