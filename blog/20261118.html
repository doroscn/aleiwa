Fault in SMW5 Submarine Cable System Causes Internet Degradation in Pakistan

2026-11-18 — Submarine Cable Outage

A fault reported in the SEA-ME-WE 5 (SMW5) submarine cable system near the coast of Pakistan has degraded national internet connectivity and reduced international transit bandwidth, as confirmed by the Pakistan Telecommunication Authority (PTA).

What We Observed

  • Pakistan Telecommunication Authority (PTA) confirmed a fault in the international submarine cable system off the coast.
  • Reports identify the affected link as part of the SEA-ME-WE 5 (SMW5) cable system.
  • Users and ISPs across Pakistan reported significant internet slowdowns and increased international latency.

Network Impact
