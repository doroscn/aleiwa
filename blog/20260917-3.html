Openreach Local Network Failure Causes Internet Outage Across Isle of Wight

2026-09-17 — Isp Outage

A major Openreach access network failure disrupted broadband services for homes and local businesses across the Isle of Wight.

What We Observed

  • Widespread loss of broadband service across multiple retail ISPs dependent on Openreach wholesale backhaul.

Network Impact

  • Significant disruption to local business transactions and consumer broadband connectivity across the Isle of Wight.

Routing Analysis

Global BGP routing for affected ISP ASNs remained active, but physical local access link failures prevented end-user packets from reaching edge routing nodes.

Possible Cause

Hardware or backhaul connectivity fault within Openreach regional access infrastructure.

Timeline

  • 2026-09-17 14:30 — Widespread broadband connectivity disruptions reported across the Isle of Wight.
  • 2026-09-17 16:00 — Openreach acknowledges local network issue as cause of the outage.

Regional Impact

Localised to broadband subscribers and businesses on the Isle of Wight.

Aleiwa Analysis

Island access topologies rely heavily on backhaul fiber links across marine bottlenecks. When wholesale physical infrastructure fails, subscriber connectivity drops even though provider core IP networks and BGP sessions remain healthy.
